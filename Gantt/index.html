Key milestones
Month 2 · May 2027
Working Group constituted
Multidisciplinary representatives from all five POGO paediatric oncology centres formally engaged
Month 6 · Sep 2027
Baseline data complete
Pre-implementation referral rates, OTC uptake, and practice variation documented across all five sites
Month 7–8 · Oct–Nov 2027
Harmonization achieved
In-person and virtual harmonization meetings completed; consensus on care pathway components reached
Month 9 · Dec 2027
Pathway published on POGO website
Harmonized OTC care pathway accessible to all five sites, satellite centres, and national network
Month 13 · Apr 2028
Full provincial implementation
All five sites — including Kingston — operating under the harmonized OTC care pathway
Month 24 · Mar 2029
Evaluation & policy brief complete
Post-implementation findings submitted for publication and compiled into Ontario Health business case policy brief
Framework: Mixed-methods evaluation guided by RE-AIM (Reach, Effectiveness, Adoption, Implementation, Maintenance), the Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research (CFIR), and Proctor's Implementation Outcomes Framework, coordinated with the concurrent OICR-funded evaluation (I2I.1448) at SickKids and Mount Sinai Fertility.  ·  Methodology: POGO care pathway harmonization model (Tomlinson et al. 2022; Yan et al. 2025; Mehrdadi et al. 2025; Mark et al. 2024).  ·  Sites: The Hospital for Sick Children (Toronto) · McMaster Children's Hospital (Hamilton) · Children's Hospital London Health Sciences Centre · Children's Hospital of Eastern Ontario (Ottawa) · Kingston Health Sciences Centre.
